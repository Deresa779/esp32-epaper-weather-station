# Enclosure

A three-part enclosure. All three parts can be 3D printed.

| File | Part | Size (mm) | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `front-plate.STL` | Front plate | 125 × 111 × 5 | Carries and protects the e-paper panel; machined from hardened PC board in the build |
| `rear-box.STL` | Rear box | 125 × 111 × 56 | Houses the controller, battery board and wiring; mounting holes on both sides |
| `lid.STL` | Lid | 105 × 56 × 3 | Flat plate with a cable pass-through slot |
| `enclosure.STEP` | Assembly | 125 × 111 × 56 | Full assembly with exact geometry, editable |

- `assembly-view.png` — assembly view
- `assembled-unit.jpg` — assembled unit with the solar panel mounted

## Key dimensions

- Panel cushioning: 8 mm × 0.5 mm (front face), 3 mm × 5 mm (recess walls), 91 × 77 × 1.5 mm (behind the panel)
- Front plate to rear box: 4 × M3 × 14 screws

For the full material notes, the foam tape sequence and the assembly order, see the
[Enclosure section of the main README](../../README.md#enclosure).
